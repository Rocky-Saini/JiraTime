NewersWorld timesheet ⇄ Jira

Agent not connected

Jira worklogs are the source. Days that are already filled, on leave, or locked in NewersWorld are never touched (unless you allow overwrite). Your NewersWorld login in the browser is used — nothing is stored here.

Connect (takes 10 seconds):
  1. Click (opens your timesheet in a new tab, you are already logged in there).
  2. In that tab click the bookmark JiraTime Agent. First time only: drag ⭐ JiraTime Agent to your bookmarks bar.
  3. Come back here. The badge turns green. (alternative: paste it in that tab's DevTools console)
